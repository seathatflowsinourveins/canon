## Findings
1. `src/canon_trading/ledger.py:205-228` (`open_study`; it opens the storage at line 219 without the new lock). Only creators take the creation lock. A process that opens the study while another process is still creating it reads a half-made study and gets `LedgerNotFound` for a study that exists a moment later. The change's own docstring (`ledger.py:190-192`) calls creation "one moment under the ledger's `.lock`", which is true for creators only. Shown by `probes/reader_race.py`: the creator was held after `optuna.create_study` and before the sample was written. `open_study` then raised `LedgerNotFound: study 's' has no sample in the form ADR 0041 gives: None` after 0.26 s. The same reader, taking a shared `flock` on the change's `trials.db.lock` first, waited out creation and opened the study after 3.26 s. The starting code fails the same way, so this is a gap left beside defect 1, not a regression. The case's own fix closes it with a shared lock in `open_study`, and the review raised it. (severity: fix)
2. `src/canon_trading/ranking.py:317-321`, with its claims at `ranking.py:24-27` and `ranking.py:80-84`. In the hour a fall-back repeats, Optuna's naive local start has lost its fold, and `astimezone(UTC)` takes the earlier instant. A trial started in the second occurrence of that hour is therefore reported one hour early, and the range differs by zone. The module header says `astimezone` "converts back". The `RankingReport` docstring says that without the conversion the ledger "would read differently in another time zone". The limit is stated only in the worker's report, marked UNVERIFIED, and not in the code or ADR 0035. Shown by `probes/fold_hour.py`: with the stored starts set to 05:30 and 06:30 UTC on 2026-11-01, Tokyo and UTC report `05:30+00:00` to `06:30+00:00`, and New York reports `05:30+00:00` to `05:30+00:00`. Optuna 5.0.0's `RDBStorage` builds every `FrozenTrial` from the model property that returns naive local time (`storages/_rdb/models.py:193-201`, `storages/_rdb/storage.py:943`), so the fold is lost before `rank` sees the value. The case's own fix uses the same expression and records the behaviour as a known limit. So what a review round would require is the recorded limit, not other code. The review's example has the wrong instant: a 05:30 UTC start (the first 01:30) reads correctly, and it is the 06:30 UTC start (01:30 EST) that New York reads an hour early. (severity: fix)
