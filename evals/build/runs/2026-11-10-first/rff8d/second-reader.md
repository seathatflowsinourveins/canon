## Findings
1. src/canon_trading/ledger.py:215-221 (used at 207 and 274). `_half_made` reads `study.direction`, and Optuna 5.0.0's `Study.direction` raises `RuntimeError` for a multi-objective study (`optuna/study/study.py:216-220`, installed). It is reached only after `SAMPLES not in user_attrs`, so for a study with no `samples` that is multi-objective, such as one another writer made under the same name in the ledger file, two calls now raise Optuna's `RuntimeError` instead of their documented errors. `open_study(name)` used to raise `LedgerNotFound` ("no sample in the form ADR 0041 gives") and `create_ledger_study(name, ...)` used to raise `DuplicatedStudyError`. I showed this with a scratch test, since removed: a ledger holding a study made with `optuna.create_study(study_name="multi", directions=["maximize","minimize"])`. `pytest.raises(LedgerNotFound)` around `open_study("multi", path)` failed with that RuntimeError, and `pytest.raises(DuplicatedStudyError)` around `create_ledger_study("multi", ...)` failed the same way. The study is still refused and nothing passes the gate, but both documented contracts break. The fix is a one-line guard (`len(study.directions) == 1` before `direction`, or compare `study.directions == [MAXIMIZE]`) plus a test. (severity: fix)
2. src/canon_trading/backtest.py:233 with src/canon_trading/ledger.py:428. The run's engine keeps `CacheConfig`'s default, and `bar_capacity` defaults to 10,000 (checked: `CacheConfig().bar_capacity` prints 10000 and `BacktestEngineConfig().cache` is None). `bars_read` then requires the count the cache holds to equal `iterations`. So every trial on a sample with more than 10,000 bars of one bar type is now told failed. Before this change such a trial recorded, for example a 1-MINUTE sample over about 26 regular-hours sessions, a bar type `bars_to_catalog` supports. This fails loudly, the report says so, and step 7's daily study (about 1,370 bars) is not affected. A review round would still have asked for one of two things. Either give the backtest engine a `CacheConfig(bar_capacity=...)` that covers the sample, which is the upstream knob, so the "bars the run read" can always be accounted for. Or state the ceiling as a limit in ADR 0041 term 3, which now names `iterations` but not the cap: the cap appears only in the module headers and an Evidence line. (severity: fix)
