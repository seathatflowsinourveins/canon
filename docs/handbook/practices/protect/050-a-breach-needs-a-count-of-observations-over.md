* A breach needs a count of observations over a span, never one quote, print or bar. Its first step is built: a breach can be a trade print, never a quote, when a window is started with `--trigger last_price`, upstream's `LAST_PRICE` election; a window started without it still releases on the first quote whose bid is at the trigger. Under a print trigger a name that stops printing is not sold, however far its bid falls, until a print, the wind-down, or 60 s with no two-sided quote in the cache (the held stop's unpriced exit; the adapter discards a one-sided quote, so a name whose quotes turn one-sided keeps its last two-sided one and waits for a print or the wind-down); a print from before the entry is no price of the holding. Under either trigger, a hold ends on the first followed price: when it, or the lowest price of the holding received since the hold began (under a print trigger, any print traded since the holding began, one reported late included), is at or under the held stop, it sells at once, and above it the trail starts from the held stop, never lower; a cheaper fill during the hold never lowers the held stop. An entry sized for L has its held stop recomputed at the distance in force below the average as it is when that price is compared, keeping any higher level held or reached; an unsized holding, or one with no room for L under the average, keeps the level it was held at. One exception keeps the hold: a followed price at or under the trailing offset or its limit offset, which the spread floor in price terms can reach, would seed a trigger at or under zero (NautilusTrader `crates/execution/src/trailing.rs:199`), so no trailing sell is sent and the stop is held until there is room, a sale through the price followed on that step or a later one or through the last known price, or the 60 s unpriced exit (`HOLD_MOST_SECS`, UNCALIBRATED), taken as above: under a print trigger only with no two-sided quote in the cache; a price already at or under a kept trigger still sells at once. A hold never keeps a stop at or under zero. The count over a span is built behind rows that are off (ADR 0119), with no window flag for them yet (roadmap step 7; ADR 0088's departures).
