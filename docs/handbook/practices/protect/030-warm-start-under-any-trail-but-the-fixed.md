* Warm start: under any trail but the fixed one, a slot's ATR warms when the slot opens, first from the cached one-minute bars of the session part now open (`_warm_atr`, `mover.py:3139`), then, if those leave it short, from one bars request of the last hour, not bounded to the part, ending 15 minutes ago on the overnight feed (`mover.py:1413-1420`, `:2926-2927`). At most 20 such requests go out a minute (`mover.py:493`). A slot still short of 14 bars, or whose request is refused or unanswered, enters on the cold path, and the window record names each outcome under `warm_up`. The cold path is ADR 0106's; the warm-up itself is the lead's ruling of 2026-11-12 and pull request 340, and no record holds it yet.
