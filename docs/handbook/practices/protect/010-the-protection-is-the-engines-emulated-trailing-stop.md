* The protection is the engine's emulated trailing stop-limit sell (`mover.py:43`; ADR 0088). Its first trigger is the planned distance under the average fill, rounded up onto the step (`fill_trigger`, `mover.py:779`), and it then follows the highest bid up, or the highest trade print in a window started with `--trigger last_price`, never down. The average is the whole holding's, every entry's fills together, as the position keeps it; a cheaper fill never lowers a stop the holding reached, and when any fill brings that average from above the kept stop to at or under it, the holding sells at once, held or trailed, while a stop already at or above the average is a winner's stop that a lower average does not cross (the lead's H3, 2026-11-13). The crossing applies to an entry sized for L and outside the persistence arms, and the warmed ATR and profile still read the entry's own fill. A Mover window that names no trail and no arm runs arm OWN1 (`session_window.py:532`, `:540`; ADR 0106): the distance is one 14-bar ATR of one-minute bars over the price, raised to two quoted spreads when that is larger, and kept over 0.02 when it is larger still, for the sizing above to carry (`mover.py:4633-4656`); sizing refuses one over 0.10 (`sizing.py:45`). `MoverConfig`'s own default, and arm F2's, is the fixed 0.02 (row `TRAIL`). Arming only on a completed confirmation above entry plus round-trip cost plus the noise floor, with an unknown cost basis or quote leaving it unarmed, is not yet built, and the requirement alone states it (roadmap step 7).
