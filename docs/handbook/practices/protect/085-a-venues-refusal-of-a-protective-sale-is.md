* A venue's refusal of a protective sale is not final: it is sent again at a paced interval with one alert for the episode, and a freeze whose cause is outside the strategy ends when that cause ends. On main the owner protector freezes a holding whose first sell is refused until the next start, and re-sends a refused sale already under way with no alert, so this is not yet built (roadmap step 7; ADR 0150 clause 3).
