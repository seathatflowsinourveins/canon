* No position exists without its exit, in any session and on any account: every long of ours carries its trailing exit from the first fill, a holding the owner opens gets the same exit by itself or is named unprotected every minute until it does, the exit is a fast sell that survives window ends, restarts and feed changes, a gain that covers its costs is locked, a protective sale arms a re-entry while the name still ranks, and the broker's own stop is only the backstop (the owner's requirement of 2026-11-13). Each of the seven clauses and what holds it is in ADR 0150, and whether each is built is printed by `python -m canon_trading.exit_rule` from the case set's own marks, never kept by hand; a landing that closes a gap removes its case's expected-failure mark and updates only its own practice file.
