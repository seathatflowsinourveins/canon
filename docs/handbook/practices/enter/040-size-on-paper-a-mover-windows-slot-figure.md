* Size on paper: a Mover window's slot figure is the smaller of the account's buying power and cash at startup over the slots, to the cent (`session_window.py:1528-1531`), and every Mover paper window sizes each entry for a planned loss through one call, `sizing.plan_for_loss` (`session_window.py:1630`, `mover.py:4244`), which calls upstream's `FixedRiskSizer` (`sizing.py:195`); the one-share bridge strategy is sized apart from this. The planned loss is the figure's whole shares times what each loses at a 0.02 stop (`PAPER_LOSS_SHARE`, `mover.py:558`, the lead's ruling of 2026-11-12; `share_loss`, `mover.py:789`); the budget is the free balance less the entries working and the buys not yet in an account report, capped at the figure, and the prior minutes' dollar flow bounds the shares (`mover.py:4177-4204`). A wider stop sizes down, a tighter one is held at the figure, and a size that cannot be made is refused by name, never estimated (`mover.py:4242-4302`). ADR 0106 states this sizing; its earlier lines that a paper Mover does not use `FixedRiskSizer` describe the code before it and no longer match `main`. A missing or invalid buying-power read refuses startup (ADR 0088, ADR 0091, ADR 0106). The Mover replay sizes from a named buying power over the slots, with no flow bound (ADR 0101).
