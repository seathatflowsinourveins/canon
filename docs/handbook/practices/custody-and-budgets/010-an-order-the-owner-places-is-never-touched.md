* An order the owner places is never touched; one writer per account and one exit per quantity, and anything foreign freezes its symbol and reports. The close by hand keeps to that: it acts only on what our client order IDs wrote and freezes and names a symbol with anything else in it (ADR 0060; `canon_trading.custody`). A replacement is read by its own ID: one our adapter sent carries the replaced order's ID with a clock stamp after its count and is ours, and one a hand made carries another ID and is foreign, whatever it replaces (ADR 0129). The account check after a window does not yet: not yet built there, and no record decides it (roadmap step 6; ADR 0060; see philosophy.md).
